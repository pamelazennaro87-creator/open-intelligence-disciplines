# Open Intelligence Disciplines

**A practical, evidence-based framework for understanding and applying open-source intelligence disciplines.**

This repository is designed for investigators, analysts, researchers and professionals who need clear distinctions between intelligence disciplines, realistic strengths and limitations, and usable methods — not theory for its own sake.

> Claims are tested, not collected.  
> Methods must remain usable under real constraints.

---

## Purpose

Most public material on intelligence disciplines is either:
- Too academic and detached from operational reality, or
- Too superficial and marketing-oriented.

This project sits in between. It aims to be:

- **Usable** – focused on what actually works in open-source environments
- **Honest** – clear about limitations, biases and confidence levels
- **Structured** – comparable frameworks across disciplines
- **Portfolio-grade** – suitable as professional reference material

---

## Core Disciplines Covered

| Discipline | Focus | Status |

## Design Principles

- **Evidence-first** – every claim must be traceable to public sources or clearly marked as assessment
- **Confidence levels** – High / Medium / Low used consistently
- **Operational relevance** – prioritise methods that remain useful under time and resource constraints
- **No hype** – avoid inflated claims about what open sources can achieve
- **Visual clarity** – diagrams and matrices where they improve understanding
